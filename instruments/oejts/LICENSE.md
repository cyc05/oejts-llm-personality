This directory contains instrument items from the **Open Extended Jungian Type Scales (OEJTS) 1.2**, authored by **Eric Jorgenson** and published via **Open Psychometrics** / personality-testing.info.

## Disclaimer

This instrument gives a result **equivalent in format** to Jungian / Myers–Briggs-style type letter codes. It is **not** the Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI®), is **not affiliated** with the Myers-Briggs Company or the MBTI assessment, and comes with **no guarantees** of reliability or accuracy of any kind.

Chinese pole wording in `items.yaml` is a controlled translation for research administration; English poles follow the OEJTS 1.2 PDF left=1 / right=5 polarity.
